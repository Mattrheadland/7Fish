# 7Fish Brand Guide

## Name
- **Product name:** 7Fish (one word in running text, app store, repo).
- **Wordmark:** "7 FISH" exactly as drawn in Matt's logo. Never retyped, never redrawn.
- **The button:** **Cast**. The result is a **Forecast**. The species vault stays the **Findex**. The log stays the **Journal**.
- Tagline options: "Should you go? Press Cast." · "Know before you load the truck." · "Fish or don't. Just don't guess."

## Logo
Matt's own 7-and-F monogram with the fish-tail sweep and orange ribbon. Use it unedited. Color changes only (seafoam on black is the master; a single-color mint or black version is allowed for small sizes and print). Keep clear space equal to the height of the ribbon on all sides. App icon is the monogram on the matte black rounded tile from the master art.

## Color (sampled from the master logo)

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--ink` | `#0B0C0C` | Matte black background |
| `--panel` | `#151818` | Cards, sheets |
| `--seafoam` | `#4ADEC8` | Primary, Cast button, GO verdict |
| `--deep-teal` | `#006A5A` | Shadows, pressed states, chart fills |
| `--mint` | `#D4F2EA` | Primary text on dark |
| `--ribbon` | `#FC7F4D` | Accent, GO IF… verdict, warnings |
| `--slate` | `#5E6866` | SKIP verdict, secondary text, disabled |

No red, white and blue combinations. SKIP is slate rather than red: skipping is a decision, not an error. Light mode inverts to mint paper with ink text; seafoam darkens to `#008C78` for contrast.

## Type
- Display and verdict: a heavy geometric sans close to the wordmark (Montserrat 800).
- Body: Inter 400/600.
- Numbers, times, gauges: JetBrains Mono, tabular figures.

## Voice
A sharp fishing buddy who reads the gauges. Short, plain, specific, a little dry. It commits to a call, admits when the data's thin, and never hypes. A dead day gets said out loud ("Front's stacked on top of you. Mow the lawn.") with the best fallback right below. One light line of humor per card, max. No exclamation points on GO.

## UI principles
- One primary action per screen. Cast is the biggest thing on the home screen.
- The verdict is readable from arm's length.
- Every number carries its label (measured, forecast, modeled, report) on tap.
- Real iOS feel: bottom tab bar (Cast · Journal · Findex · Spots), sheets for detail, haptic tick on Cast.
